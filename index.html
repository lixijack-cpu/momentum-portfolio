About This Strategy ▼

A systematic long-only US equity strategy that selects stocks monthly using a two-factor ranking model — earnings surprise (67%) and valuation (33%) — combined at selection with an alternative-data price signal. Portfolio construction applies convex drawdown-aware optimization with dynamic regime-based risk management, allowing the strategy to reduce equity exposure during adverse market conditions.

The model deliberately carries fewer factors than it once did. An analyst-revision signal was removed in Aug 2026 after out-of-sample testing showed it did not predict returns, and two further candidate factors (residual momentum and earnings acceleration) were built, measured and rejected on the same evidence. Factors are added here only when they survive that test, and the current two-factor blend has not yet cleared statistical significance against its predecessor either.

Validated using point-in-time clean data spanning 2005–2026 — no look-ahead bias, no survivorship bias. Live paper trading commenced July 2026.

This is a personal research project and paper trading simulation. Historical performance represents a point-in-time simulation; no live capital was deployed prior to July 2026. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Not investment advice.
