About This Strategy ▼

A live, fully automated systematic equity research platform I architected from scratch — covering the full stack from raw data ingestion to public dashboard deployment. The core engine ranks ~1,500 U.S. equities monthly using a composite multi-factor signal: post-earnings announcement drift (SUE), earnings quality, valuation, and price momentum — all computed point-in-time to eliminate lookahead bias. A macro-regime risk overlay governs market exposure.

Three execution tracks run simultaneously, each certified on a 20-year out-of-sample walk-forward (2005–2026):

  • Retail (Live Paper Trading): Concentrated 10-stock model optimised for low-capital accounts with integer-share execution. Walk-forward: 17.11% CAGR / Sharpe 0.921 / +607 bps/yr vs S&P 500 / −29.6% max drawdown. Currently executing in a live $10,000 paper account.
  • Institutional: Flagship 104-stock equal-weighted model simulating a $5M AUM book. Walk-forward: 14.00% CAGR / Sharpe 0.942 / +296 bps/yr vs S&P 500 / −19.5% max month-end drawdown.
  • Shadow: Forward-testing laboratory for candidate alpha signals evaluated strictly out-of-sample before promotion to the live book.

The full pipeline — automated CRSP/Compustat data ingestion, point-in-time factor scoring, pre-trade risk checks, nightly signal integrity monitoring, and continuous GitHub Pages dashboard deployment — is written entirely in Python and executes autonomously every market close.
